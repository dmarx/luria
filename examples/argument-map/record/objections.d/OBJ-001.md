# OBJ-001: The inspection predates the traffic increase

The 2025 inspection measured a deck carrying 2023 traffic. An absence of
cracks then says little about the deck now, so the step from [CLAIM-007](../claims.d/CLAIM-007.md) to
[CLAIM-006](../claims.d/CLAIM-006.md) does not hold, and the uncontested page leaves it out.
