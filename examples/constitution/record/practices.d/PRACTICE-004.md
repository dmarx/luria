# PRACTICE-004: Sign each conversational claim with its source inline

**Superseded.** The intent was right — a reader should be able to tell a
measurement from a recollection — and the mechanism was wrong: annotating
every sentence made the ones that mattered invisible among the ones that did
not.

Left in place rather than deleted, for the reason any retired document is: it
is the target of [BOUNDARY-002](../boundaries.d/BOUNDARY-002.md), and an
override pointing at nothing is an override nobody can check. A record that
deletes what it supersedes loses the ability to say what changed.
