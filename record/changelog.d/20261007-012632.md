### Fixed

- A symmetric relation that crosses schemes — one field name on both sides,
  each naming the other's scheme — is read from both sides again. The two
  sides' readings were keyed by field name, so the far side's overwrote the
  near side's: a pair held on both sides was reported as one-sided from both
  ends, and `luria link --fix` could not settle it. Same-scheme symmetric
  relations were unaffected. The shape was declarable before multi-scheme
  references, which made it easier to reach ([#160](https://github.com/dmarx/luria/issues/160)).
