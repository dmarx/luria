### Documentation

- `docs/paper/`: a conceptual paper, *The Architecture of a Living Corpus*,
  arguing that a corpus is living when it can change its mind accountably, with
  Luria as the motivating example and this record as the case study. Twelve
  section files, a reference list, and a pandoc recipe in its README; listed in
  the docs map under a new "Essays" heading. Its citations of the record are
  checked like any other page's.
- Nine decisions the paper cites gained the `docs` tag (the convention for
