### Added

- A chain's `scheme:` takes a list ([ADR-tmp5vjuy](record/decisions.d/ADR-tmp5vjuy.md)). Any listed scheme's documents may be a step. A walked field may be declared on, and land on, any listed scheme, and a walked pattern may start and end on any of them. A string is a list of one, so existing chains are unchanged.

### Changed

- The refusal for a chain relation that can leave the chain's schemes now names both remedies: list the scheme in `scheme`, or declare `invariant` on the reference.
