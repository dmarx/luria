### Added

- **This record now shows a map of itself.** `[luria.site] graph` points at
  `docs/graphs/record-map.json` — the whole record, 248 nodes and 482 typed
  edges, drawn in strata-g from the record itself. Nothing filtered: a map
  that quietly dropped documents would be one you cannot trust, and density is
  what the viewer's zoom, search and Fit are for. Documents with a code link to
  their page; a journal entry or changelog fragment has none, so its node does
  not navigate. [`docs/graphs/README.md`](../../docs/graphs/README.md) has the
  recipe for redrawing it.
