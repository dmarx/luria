---
title: 'Relation statements move into a ref:: namespace'
created: '2026-09-30T05:17:04'
tags: []
---

**Bare field names as directive names were one namespace too few.**
Naming a relation directive by its field (`<!-- extends: X -->`) put
user-defined names in the same space as the fixed vocabulary. That cost two
things.
- **A refusal list in `luria.yaml`.** No field could be called `pin`,
  anything ending in `-ok`, and so on.
- **A blind spot.** A misspelt field could not be told apart from an
  unrelated comment, so it was silently nothing.

Review suggested a prefix, and `<!-- ref::extends: X -->` removes both
costs. Every `ref::` directive is a relation claim, so `ref::extnds` is now a
`bad-annotations` finding. The only names still refused are the ones ending
in `-block` or `-file`, because a scope is a suffix on the name.

**The grammar change is a namespace prefix on the name.** `DIRECTIVE_RE` and
`SHAPED_RE` accept an optional `[a-z][\w-]*::` ahead of the name. The scope
suffix stays at the end (`ref::extends-block:`), so the rule "scope is a
suffix before the colon" holds for every directive. `shaped_spans` split the
name at the first `:` and would have read `ref::extends` as `ref`. It now
splits at the first colon that is not half of a `::`. Nothing in the suite
or this repo's lint moved.

**Fired on `examples/constitution` again.** The counts matched the previous
round: 4 statements written, 1 pushed up, and 6 unexplained after a `-file`
statement with a reason. A hand-added `<!-- ref::grunds: VALUE-4 -->` came
back as `ref::grunds names no reference field this document holds`.
