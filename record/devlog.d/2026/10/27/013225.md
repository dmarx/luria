---
title: "Chains over several schemes, fired on nucleation's dialectic"
created: '2026-10-27T01:32:25'
tags: []
---

**The problem.** dmarx/nucleation's dialectic chain walked `objects_to`
between claims. Its objections to inferences `undercuts` an ARG instead,
so five of its nine undercutting claims were on no thread. A pattern,
`(u:CLAIM)-[:undercuts]->(g:ARG)-[:concludes]->(c:CLAIM)`, placed them
under the conclusion "through" the ARG. But no pattern could place an ARG
that nothing undercuts, because a chain step had to start and end on the
chain's own scheme. The owner's reading was that patterns exist to express
paths that are not homogeneous. They already match such paths. The chain
was what refused the step.

**The fix.** A chain's `scheme:` takes a list ([ADR-tmp5vjuy](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-tmp5vjuy.md)). The walker
loads every listed scheme, reads each walked field on every listed scheme
that declares it, and keeps the targets that landed in the list. The
consistency rules are restated over the list, and they reduce to the old
ones when it has a single entry. A step links into its own scheme's
directory and reads its facets on its own scheme's contract, so an
argument in a thread of claims shows its status and no voice.

**Fired on the real case** (2026-10-27). With nucleation's dialectic set
to `scheme: [CLAIM, ARG]` and `relation: [objects_to, undercuts,
concludes]`, and no pattern, `luria index` drew 49 lines, up from 48 with
the pattern and 46 before it. All ten ARGs are steps. ARG-005 and ARG-009,
which nothing undercuts, appear under CLAIM-027, which they conclude. Each
undercut nests under its argument, for example CLAIM-086, then ARG-003,
then CLAIM-032. The nucleation change waits for a release carrying this.

**What the walk did not need.** [ADR-106](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-106.md) rejected carrying far-scheme
nodes partly because it would need "a new rule for depth and ordering at
a node with no outgoing spine". It needed none. A node with no outgoing
spine is a root, and `_order` already emits roots.

**A trap in the test run, not in the change.** Two tests in
`tests/test_encoding.py` fail in a fresh cloud container with
`ModuleNotFoundError: jinja2`. They shell out to an interpreter that does
not see the editable install's dependencies, and they fail identically on
a clean checkout.
