**The question.** [ADR-127](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-127.md) keeps the logic core to stratified Datalog with
exactly one answer set. `luria/logic/__init__.py` refuses any other count,
because a view that depended on the model the solver found first would not
be a function of the sources. That leaves out the half of clingo that
answer-set programming is for: choice rules, integrity constraints and
optimisation, which search for an assignment rather than derive one. The
owner asked what that half could do. dmarx/nucleation had a ready case. On
2026-11-01 it made `line:` required for CASE and inferred the nine missing
values by hand, from a SQL query over `luria export` plus judgement ([NUC-ADR-067](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/blob/main/record/decisions.d/ADR-067.md)).

**The determinism answer.** Several optimal answers do not make the output
depend on search order, provided what is read back is not "the model
found". `--opt-mode=optN` with `--enum-mode=cautious` gives the atoms true
in every optimal answer, and `--enum-mode=brave` gives those true in some.
Both are functions of the input. Cautious atoms are safe to apply; brave
atoms that are not cautious are a choice for a person.

**What was built** (`experiments/asp_propose/`, outside the package): a
program of about 20 rules over `relations.lp`'s `held/4` and the `value/4`
facts, parameterised by scheme and field.

- `may(C, V)`: the values a document with an empty field could take. They
  come from its valued neighbours, or recursively from what an empty
  neighbour could take. This is plain Datalog, computed before any guess.
- A choice rule picks some of them, with at least one where there is any
  candidate.
- An integrity constraint says every neighbour that has, or is given, a
  value must share one with the document.
- `#minimize` counts values.
- `no_evidence(C)` names the empty documents with no candidate at all, so
  the silent case is reported ([DP-015](../../record/principles.d/DP-015.md)).

The optimum is 8 values. [NUC-CASE-007](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/blob/main/record/cases.d/CASE-007.md) and [NUC-CASE-028](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/blob/main/record/cases.d/CASE-028.md) resolve only through

left them out. On the record as merged, where nothing is empty, the
proposal is empty.

**Fire before trusting ([DP-006](../../record/principles.d/DP-006.md)).** The JSON is byte-identical across
`--seed` 0, 1, 7 and 42 with `--rand-freq=0.5`, and under
`--parallel-mode=4`. The whole run takes about 6 s, most of it
`luria.facts`.

**What this does not settle.** There is no applier: `luria relate --draft`
writes reference fields only, and `line` is a vocabulary field.
`field_edit.add_to_field` would do the edit. The constraint ("share a value
with every valued neighbour") is one reasonable reading of what `line`
means; another record's field could want "share with most". Only one field
on one record was tried. Repairs for invariant findings, the fewest tag
additions that clear `unbound_edge`, are the obvious second case and were
not tried. A Proposed ADR for a `propose` mode follows from this entry.
