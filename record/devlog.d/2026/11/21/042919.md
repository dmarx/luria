The guard of [ADR-143](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-143.md), fired on the real case that prompted it, from
this repository's code: `python -m luria.cli new adr --title "Revisits
ADR-001 on naming"` refused with the message, and wrote nothing. Before the
change the same title was scaffolded (with the installed release, which
does not have the guard). The tests cover the refusal, a code in backticks
passing, and a code-shaped word from no scheme passing.

Two tests in `tests/test_encoding.py` fail in the environment this was
written in, on a missing `jinja2`, on `main` as well; they are not this
change's.
